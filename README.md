## 1. Debit & Credit Transaction Analysis
**File:** `Debit_and_Credit_banking_data_new_2_.xlsx`

**Goal:** Compare credits against debits and flag high-risk and suspicious transactions.

**Data:** 100,000 transactions from Jan to Dec 2024 (INR), across 6 branches and 3 transaction methods (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer).

**Workbook contents**
- `Credit_Debit_Data`: raw data
- Question sheets 1 to 12 with Pivot Tables, including a High-Risk vs Normal comparison and suspicious-transaction analysis
- `DASHBOARD`: summary sheet with Slicers

**Quick facts**
- About 50/50 split between credit (50,028) and debit (49,972) transactions
- Total credits of about ₹127.6M against debits of about ₹127.3M
- About 10.4% of transactions are flagged High Risk

## 2. Bank Loan Portfolio Analysis
**File:** `dashboard.xlsx`

**Goal:** Track loan portfolio health across states, branches and regions.

**Data:** About 65.5K loan accounts (FY2017 to FY2020), 52 columns, total loan amount of about ₹751M.

**Workbook contents**
- `Banking Data`: raw data
- Pivot Table sheets: `1 to 7`, `8 to 10`, `11 to`
- `Branch Wise Analysis`
- `Dashboard`: summary sheet with Slicers

**Quick facts**
- 1,020 default loans (about 1.6%)
- 7,106 delinquent loans (about 10.8%)
